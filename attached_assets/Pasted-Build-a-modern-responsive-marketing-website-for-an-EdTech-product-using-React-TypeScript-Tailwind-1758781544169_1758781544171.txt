Build a modern, responsive marketing website for an EdTech product using React, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, and shadcn/ui components. 
The website should look professional, clean, and appealing to both students (grades 8–12) and their parents/teachers. 

Product Concept:
The platform leverages students’ handwritten answer sheets (scanned or uploaded) to derive deeper insights into their learning patterns. 
It evaluates not just marks but conceptual understanding, ability to recall formulas, accuracy in mathematical calculations, reading comprehension, 
and how well they interpret questions. Based on these factors, the system generates personalized reports highlighting strengths and improvement 
areas in each subject.

Target Audience:
- Students between grades 8 and 12
- Parents who want detailed progress insights
- Schools and coaching institutes that want to adopt better assessment analytics

Pages & Sections to Include:
1. **Home Page**:
   - Hero section with product tagline like: 
     "Transform Answer Sheets into Insights: Unlock Every Student’s True Potential."
   - Call-to-action button: “Get Early Access” / “Request a Demo”.
   - Eye-catching illustration or animation of handwritten sheets turning into a dashboard.

2. **Features Page**:
   - Cards/sections that explain features:
     • Concept Understanding Analysis  
     • Formula Recall Tracking  
     • Accuracy in Calculations  
     • Reading & Question Interpretation  
     • Personalized Strengths & Weakness Reports  
   - Include icons or visuals for each.

3. **How It Works**:
   - Step-by-step flow:
     Upload Handwritten Sheet → AI Processing → Insights Dashboard → Personalized Report.
   - Include simple graphics or a timeline component.

4. **Why It Matters (Impact Section)**:
   - For Students: Better self-awareness, focused improvement.  
   - For Parents: Clearer picture beyond grades.  
   - For Schools: Data-driven teaching and targeted support.  

5. **Testimonials / Case Study Section** (placeholder for now).

6. **Pricing (Placeholder)**:
   - Basic (free demo), Premium (personalized reports), Institutional Plan.

7. **Contact / Demo Form**:
   - Simple form (name, email, role: student/parent/teacher, message).
   - Button: “Schedule a Demo”.

8. **Footer**:
   - Links to About, Contact, Privacy Policy.
   - Social icons.

Design Style:
- Clean, modern, minimal with academic/tech aesthetic.
- Soft color palette (blue, green, white).
- Rounded card components with subtle shadows.
- Use shadcn/ui for forms, cards, buttons, navbar, and testimonial carousel.
- Mobile-friendly and responsive.
